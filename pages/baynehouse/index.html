Seeking Private Academic Tutor / Educator

Bothell, WA | In Person | $50–$75/hour | 6–8 hours/week

We are a family in Bothell, Washington, seeking an enthusiastic, capable, and patient educator to provide individualized academic instruction for our 13-year-old son, Remy. This is a long-term, part-time opportunity for someone who enjoys working one-on-one with students and is interested in helping shape a personalized educational experience.

Our son previously attended a small alternative school where he received approximately 8.5 hours of individual tutoring each week. We’re now looking to establish a similar arrangement directly with a private tutor.

About the Student and Educational Goals

Our son is following a nontraditional educational path, with the long-term goal of completing his high-school education early (likely through the GED) and eventually attending art school.

We’re looking for someone who can help him build a foundation across core academic subjects while developing the independent learning skills he’ll need to succeed in his future studies.

This is not primarily a homework-help or test-preparation position. We’re hoping to find someone who can build an ongoing educational relationship with our son, adapt instruction to his needs and interests, and help guide his academic progress over time.

Responsibilities

  • Provide one-on-one instruction across core academic subjects, including mathematics, English/language arts, science, and social studies.
  • Assess his current knowledge, strengths, and areas for improvement.
  • Help develop and follow a personalized learning plan, with an emphasis on building toward high-school-level proficiency.
  • Select or recommend appropriate learning materials and assignments.
  • Encourage independent learning, critical thinking, and effective work habits.
  • Monitor progress and communicate periodically with parents about goals, achievements, and areas needing additional attention.

We don’t expect one person to be an expert in every subject, but we’re looking for someone comfortable teaching across multiple disciplines and helping coordinate a well-rounded education.

Schedule and Compensation

  • Hours: Approximately 6–8 hours per week, plus 1 hour paid prep time per week.
  • Sessions: Two-hour sessions, three to four days per week.
  • Scheduling: Flexible. We can work with the right candidate to establish a consistent schedule.
  • Location: In-person instruction at our home in Bothell, Washington.
  • Compensation: $50–$75 per hour, depending on qualifications and experience.
  • Duration: Ongoing, with the hope of establishing a long-term relationship.

Qualifications

We’re particularly interested in candidates who have:

  • Experience tutoring, teaching, mentoring, or otherwise working academically with middle- or high-school-aged students.
  • The ability to adapt teaching approaches to an individual student’s learning style.
  • Patience, curiosity, enthusiasm, and the ability to build a positive relationship with a young learner.
  • The ability to work independently and take initiative in planning lessons.

Additional qualifications that are welcome, but not required:

  • A degree or coursework in education or a related field.
  • Experience with homeschooling, alternative education, or individualized learning programs.
  • Experience designing curricula or managing a student’s long-term academic progress.
  • Familiarity with GED preparation or accelerated educational pathways.

We welcome applications from experienced professional tutors, current or former teachers, graduate students, and advanced undergraduate students with relevant teaching or tutoring experience.
